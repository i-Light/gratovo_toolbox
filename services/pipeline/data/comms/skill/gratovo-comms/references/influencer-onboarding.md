# Influencers — Onboarding

### INF-ONB-01 — Tell them what happens next — no big promises

Rule (must): Right after closing, tell the creator the next step in a way that fits how you presented yourself (agency representing them, checking with brands, or exclusive representation) and keep it warm without promising deals.
Source: 2.8. Closed an influencer, now what — "These updates are professional and keep the relationship warm, without making any big promises."
Source: lesson video "2.8. Closed an influencer, now what" @4:08 — "And just stay more transparent and more communicative with them because they actually trust you with just being you managing them exclusively."

### INF-ONB-02 — Set the timeline and check in

Rule (must): Tell them outreach usually takes 1-3 weeks; if 3+ weeks pass without a deal, reassure them that outreach continues; send short check-ins even when nothing has moved so there is never long silence. This is the default of house choice HC-29; the alternative is INF-ONB-07.
Source: 2.8. Closed an influencer, now what — "It’s normal for outreach to take 1-3 weeks as we match the right brand with the right influencer."
Source: 2.8. Closed an influencer, now what — "Even if there's no movement, drop a quick update to avoid silence: "Just a quick check-in-we're still in the outreach phase and will update you as soon as something comes up!""

### INF-ONB-03 — An eager creator who keeps chasing

When: Only cite brands saying no when that really happened.
Rule (tip): If a creator checks in very often, reassure them professionally; their eagerness is useful later: when brands balk at the budget, propose a lower per-video rate framed as more deals and more volume for them.
Don't: Ask an eager creator for a lower rate claiming brands said no when the agency has not actually pitched them
Source: 2.8. Closed an influencer, now what — "If an influencer gets impatient and keeps checking in, this might reveal their neediness."
Source: 2.8. Closed an influencer, now what — "We've got a few brands saying no already due to budget contraints, we may need to review your pricing options and see if we can adjust to fit in with the current brand's budgets. This means less money per deal, but way more deals, allowing you to overall have more ressources to invest in your channel, it's just a question of volume"

### INF-ONB-04 — Lock the creator's side of a sold deal

Rule (must): Once a brand buys, confirm with the creator: the earliest posting date (with the brand's deadline in the message), a shipping address if a product is sent, and whether they need product details or guidelines.
Source: 3.1. Brand accepted, now what — "Posting Date: - Confirm when they're available to post the content. Example message: "Hey Mr Beast, what's the soonest you can have this live? The brand's looking to finalize the campaign before October 31st, so let me know what works for you !"
Source: 3.1. Brand accepted, now what — "If the brand needs to send them a product, collect their shipping details."

### INF-ONB-05 — Know the creator is available and willing before pitching them

Rule (must): Stay in regular touch with every creator on the roster and know their availability (and any brands they would refuse) before putting them in front of a brand. A brand that says yes to a creator who then declines damages the brand relationship.
Source: lesson video "8.Abu Lahya Reviewing Emails" @18:14 — "So I pitched the guy to the brand. The brand accepted him. I went back to him. He was like, no. Honestly, that was quite hard, and it affected the relationship of the brand, definitely. So, be and this is something I really want you guys to do if you're reaching out to brands. Always like update with the influencer. Have like be updated with him. Like, when are you available?"
Source: coaching call "July 9th: Review My Negotiations - Kamil" (2026-07-09) — "you're too scared of letting the brand wait a little bit as soon as a brand express interest for an influencer jump to that influencer directly hey we got this brand we got this deal what do you think directly instantly"

### INF-ONB-06 — A student's experience: closed creators wait — keep them updated

Rule (tip): Creators do not lose interest because a brand takes weeks or months to find. Send a short update now and then (still pitching brands, will be back as soon as there is something) and they stay ready.
Source: lesson video "6.How Abdelmenan Closed 4,500 on his first deal" @29:31 — "I have a couple influencers. I still haven't got a deal for them and it's been about two months."
Source: coaching call "November 10th 2025: Influencer Q&A" (2025-11-10) — "Updating them is not a bad idea. You can be like, hey, I reached out to this and that brand. We had a few connections regarding you, but none of them at the budget. I'll keep you updated on these next brands, you know."

### INF-ONB-07 — No timeframe and no empty updates to a newly closed creator

Rule (should): Once you hold a creator's details and price, close with a plain 'thanks, I'll pitch you to brands such as X and get back to you as soon as there's a fitting opportunity', with no timeframe: a promised date becomes a debt, and managers chase it every week. The next message they need is a deal or real news (which brands we pitched and what they said). Do not write just to say there is no deal yet. Regular updates make sense once you have done deals together. One of the options of house choice HC-29; the default is the lesson's INF-ONB-02.
Source: coaching call "December 22nd: Influencers Q&A - Younes" (2025-12-22) — "But if you've just gotten these details and that's it and no really like close connections, then I just wait until they actually have a deal."
Source: coaching call "December 22nd: Influencers Q&A - Younes" (2025-12-22) — "As long as you have the information and you can get back to him when you have a deal, he's going to be happy, whether it's December, January, or whatever. It doesn't matter."

### INF-ONB-08 — A closed creator asks for news: tell the truth

Rule (must): When a closed creator asks how it is going, answer honestly: which brands passed and the real reason (price, audience fit), that we are working on it, and roughly when they will hear more. Never invent progress.
Source: coaching call "31/7" (2025-07-31) — "just whatever is true, just say whether it was overpriced, whether it wasn't, whether it was audience, whatever. Just be open, be transparent and say that we'll let you know in the upcoming weeks if there is more interest or not."
Source: coaching call "November 24th: Influencers Q&A - Younes" (2025-11-24) — "hey, it's been a bit back and forth, ended up not being a good fit, we're currently working on why, so we can make sure that you will have something within the next few weeks"
