# Brands — Closing

### BRD-CLOSE-01 — Once the brand says yes: lock the details

Rule (must): Confirm with the brand: the main talking points and call to action (e.g. a discount code), the posting deadline (do not ask again if they already gave one), whether they approve content before posting, and any hashtags, links or tags.
Don't: Ask again and again for extras the brief and the contract leave out (hashtags, tags): if neither requires it, the creator need not do it
Source: 3.1. Brand accepted, now what — "Main Talking points : - What do they want the influencer to highlight in the campaign? Example: Key features of a product, benefits, unique selling points, or a specific call-to-action (e.g., "Tell the audience to use this code for 20% off"). - Make sure they're crystal clear about what they expect."
Source: 3.1. Brand accepted, now what — "Any Additional Requirements: - Do they want to approve the content before posting? - Any hashtags, links, or tags they want included? - Clarify everything upfront to avoid miscommunication later."

### BRD-CLOSE-02 — Interest is not money: re-confirm the creator

Rule (must): When the brand says yes, go back to the creator first: confirm availability for the window and the rate (and, if you did not negotiate hard before, try to shave it slightly).
Source: 4.1. Working With the Brand - Coach Ammar — "Interest isn't money on the table. Treat it like someone walking into your shoe store and asking to see a few pairs. Go back to the creator, confirm they're still available for the window, and re-confirm the rate."
Source: lesson video "4.1. Working With the Brand - Coach Ammar" @9:32 — "You have to confirm three things. Are they interested in a campaign? They have available for Go Live window. So they have free slots, right? And their rates."

### BRD-CLOSE-03 — Rate, deliverables and go-live date in writing before any contract

Rule (must): Agree the rate, the deliverables and the go-live date with the brand in writing before anything is signed.
Source: 4.1. Working With the Brand - Coach Ammar — "Rate, deliverables, and go-live date all get agreed in writing with the brand before anything gets signed."
