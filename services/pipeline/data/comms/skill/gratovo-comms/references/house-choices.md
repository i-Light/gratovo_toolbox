# House choices

Where the course gives more than one answer, the agency's pick (or the default until it picks) applies.

## HC-01 — Automated follow-ups on a cold first message
Applies: 2-3 touches, then stop (2026 deliverability update: long sequences now hurt the domain)
Owner's rule: The owner's answers to HC-01 (2-3 touches) and HC-17 (3-4 follow-ups) meet at three: send at most three follow-ups after the first email, then stop or start a new thread with a new angle.

## HC-02 — Daily sending cap per mailbox
Applies: 35 outreach/day per inbox unless warmed 3+ weeks with strong stats (warm-up lesson)
Owner's rule: 35 cold emails a day per mailbox by default; the owner can set each mailbox between 10 and 50 in the app.

## HC-03 — Warm-up length before cold sending
Applies: 14 days minimum (warm-up lesson)
Owner's rule: 14 days is the minimum; 30-90 days is better. Warm-up runs in Instantly.ai, outside the app.

## HC-04 — Agency share
Applies: Broker: buy the creator below market, sell 30-70% higher (pricing masterclass, 3 ways to make money)
Owner's rule: Broker by default. For an unmanaged creator with exceptional potential (no agent; strong views or engagement for their size), keep doing discounted deals first; after 2-3 deals that prove we deliver, a person may propose exclusive management at 20-40% (about 25% when starting) on every deal (INF-MGR-05, INF-MGR-01, FND-MIND-12). Never mention a commission otherwise.

## HC-05 — Niche CPM table
Applies: Owner's table, the widest of both course ranges: tech 20-50, finance 15-80, fitness 10-35, entertainment 5-20, gaming 10-20, beauty 10-25, travel 12-20, education 25-40, food 10-20, luxury 30-50 (USD CPM); a person picks the number

## HC-06 — Subject lines for first emails
Applies: Short curiosity lines or a detail pulled from the personalization; never 'Partnership opportunity' patterns (Coach Ammar)

## HC-07 — Emergency social proof
Applies: Yes: 'part of a group of experienced agency owners who review our work' is true

## HC-08 — Female and couple creators
Applies: Avoid female creators entirely, couple channels included (owner, stricter than the course)
Owner's rule: Do not contact or pitch female creators or couple channels; a channel flagged this way is out of scope.

## HC-09 — Boycotted brands
Applies: Boycott the brands/countries the owner lists
Owner's rule: Reject any brand from Israel and any brand that supports Israel against Palestine; the agency's list of boycotted brands lives in the app's Settings. A boycotted brand gets no pitch and no creator for it.

## HC-10 — Straight cash offers to creators
Applies: Not yet: never propose straight cash offers
Owner's rule: No straight cash offers by default. A person may switch it on for a single case; the workflow never proposes one on its own.

## HC-11 — Representing a creator to a brand
Applies: Yes, once the creator has agreed to this deal in writing (true statement)

## HC-12 — Influencer follow-up spacing
Applies: 1 working day after a first email with no reply; 2 working days after a stalled exchange (follow-up lesson)

## HC-13 — When to negotiate the creator's price
Applies: Both: test their flexibility at onboarding, then ask once more, honestly, when a real deal arrives (Coaches Ammar, Younes, Osama)

## HC-14 — What the first email to a brand contains
Applies: Name the creator with one key fact, the gap, an idea and a call to action (How to Build a Strong Pitch; Coaches Younes, Michael, Kushtrim and Ammar on live calls)

## HC-15 — What a follow-up carries
Applies: A light first nudge, then change or add something true in each later one, since a seen-and-ignored email is not answered by a bump (Coaches Abu Numaan, Younes, Osama, Ammar)

## HC-16 — How many creators to close before brand outreach
Applies: 4-5 creators, with media kits and negotiated prices (Coach Younes, on four calls: 'the course might need a small update')

## HC-17 — How many follow-ups to a creator who has not replied
Applies: Three or four follow-ups (Coach Younes; Google now penalises long cold sequences)
Owner's rule: The owner's answers to HC-01 (2-3 touches) and HC-17 (3-4 follow-ups) meet at three: send at most three follow-ups after the first email, then stop or start a new thread with a new angle.

## HC-18 — Largest creators to approach without a brand in hand
Applies: No cap: anyone with about 50k average views (Coach Younes)

## HC-19 — When to pitch a brand its next creator
Applies: After the results are in and good (upsell lessons; some coaches wait about three weeks)

## HC-20 — How a cold email ends
Applies: One short yes-or-no question (the lesson; Coaches Ammar and Dylan)

## HC-21 — A brand asks for creators we don't have yet
Applies: Reply promptly: we're checking, or nothing matching right now, back as soon as we have one (Coaches Osama, Younes, Kushtrim, Michael)

## HC-22 — When the rate goes to a brand
Applies: With the details: channel, analytics, rate, availability; a direct price question gets the price (Coaches Dylan, Younes, Abu Numaan)

## HC-23 — A pitched creator falls through
Applies: Same email: the plain news and a ready replacement at a similar price (Coaches Osama, Younes, Kushtrim, Ahmed)

## HC-24 — Brand follow-up spacing
Applies: Two or three days, then about three, then a week: every two days is too aggressive with brands (Coaches Himzo, Ammar)

## HC-25 — Several people at one brand
Applies: Two at a time, each email worded for its reader, never naming each other; then the next two (Coaches Ammar, Kushtrim and others)

## HC-26 — Priced options in an offer to a brand
Applies: Two or three priced deliverables so the brand can choose, one clearly the strongest (brand lesson)

## HC-27 — Audience share needed to pitch a creator
Applies: About 30% in the brand's countries is good, less still workable; in tech, 20%+ is fine; awareness brands take less when views are big (Coaches Younes, Izak)

## HC-28 — Tone toward a creator
Applies: Very casual, matching the creator's style and language; more professional from the follow-up (Coach Ammar)

## HC-29 — Timeline and updates after closing a creator
Applies: Say outreach usually takes 1-3 weeks and send short check-ins even when nothing has moved (influencer lesson)

## HC-30 — Whitelisting and the usual exclusivity in a creator deal
Applies: Ask the creator whether they accept and at what price before answering the brand (Coaches Ammar, Younes and others)

## HC-31 — A product sent with a paid deal
Applies: Only a very expensive product may count toward the price, with cash up front always (brand lesson)

## HC-32 — Faith phrases in business messages
Applies: Leave them out, and greet plainly in cold messages (Coach Ammar and a call example of a creator put off)

## HC-33 — When the agency's content requirements reach the creator
Applies: With the deal terms, in the email and the contract, before filming (Coaches Eyoub, Kushtrim, Younes, Ammar)

## HC-34 — Instagram reel price
Applies: About half the YouTube CPM (lesson 2.4/2.5, INF-PRICE-04)
