# Brands — Delivery

### BRD-DELIV-01 — Go to them: update the brand at every step

Rule (must): After the yes, the brand hears from you at each stage without asking: working on the draft, when the draft comes, the draft for review, the go-live date, live, and analytics within 30 days. Brands do not hate emails, only useless ones.
Source: 3.1. Brand accepted, now what — "The main thing you need to do is go to them—keep them updated at every step."
Source: 3.1. Brand accepted, now what — "Stop thinking that brands hate receiving emails. What they hate is receiving emails that aren’t helpful or don’t provide real value."

### BRD-DELIV-02 — Campaign update rhythm

Rule (must): During a campaign, email the brand every 2-5 days depending on the deadline, even when nothing changed (say what you are doing and when the next update comes). Milestones: draft ready for review, planned posting date (last checks), posted, performance at about 24 h and 48-72 h. If a date slips, tell the brand straight away: a short apology and the new date, without blame or a long story.
Source: 3.1. Brand accepted, now what — "Keep them updated at each stage, with an email every 2-5 days depending on the campaign deadline."
Source: 3.1. Brand accepted, now what — "Keep them posted on the post performance (24h after, 48-72 hours after, just to hype them up)"

### BRD-DELIV-03 — Keep the brand's tone, keep the creator authentic

Rule (should): Brands want consistent messaging across campaigns (playful, professional, inspirational...); help the creator adapt to that tone without losing what makes their content trusted.
Source: 3.3. Brands and their objectives — "Help influencers adjust their messaging to align with the brand’s image while maintaining authenticity."
Source: lesson video "3.3. Brands and their objectives" @13:31 — "It's taking the same brand, you're adapting the angles to the influencer that you have in front of you. And that's just to make sure the brand looks understood and looks legit, and they have consistency in the way they're being promoted."

### BRD-DELIV-04 — Write down the brand's requirements

Rule (must): Before production, capture the brand's message, target audience, campaign goal, content guidelines (logos, call to action, placement) and approval process; if the brand did not provide a document, the agency writes one and uses it with both sides.
Source: 3.3. Service delivery — "Create a document outlining these requirements if the brand hasn’t already done so."

### BRD-DELIV-05 — Draft, approval, revisions — through the agency

Rule (must): Send the creator's draft to the brand and ask for changes; pass revision requests to the creator quickly; send the updated draft back for final approval. When the contract gives the brand a review period before a fixed go-live date, a polite note that later edits may not be possible (or may cost extra) if no feedback comes by then is fair.
Do: If the brand still has not approved a draft after follow-ups and go-live is close, escalate: write to a more senior contact you have, say what the deadline is, and ask for the approval so the video can go live on time
Source: 3.3. Service delivery — "Send the influencer’s draft to the brand for feedback and approval."
Source: 3.3. Service delivery — "If the brand asks for revisions or changes to the content, it’s important to communicate this back to the influencer quickly."

### BRD-DELIV-06 — Get the brief and read it first

Rule (must): Once everything is signed, ask the brand for the brief, read it yourself, settle any gaps with the brand, and later check the creator's draft against it before the brand sees it.
Source: 4.1. Working With the Brand - Coach Ammar — "Once everything is signed, ask the brand for the brief. Read it yourself before forwarding it, resolve any gaps with the brand, and check the returned draft against it before it ever reaches the brand."

### BRD-DELIV-07 — Brand assets with haram elements: replace or decline

Rule (must): If a brand sends footage with women or other haram elements, reject it, ask for halal alternatives, and decline the job if they refuse.
Source: 1.8. Halal & Haram Aspects of IMA — "What if a Brand Sends Footage With Women or Other Haram Elements? You must: 1. Reject the footage 2. Request Halal alternatives 3. Decline the job if they refuse"

### BRD-DELIV-08 — Never change the brand's brief behind its back

Rule (must): If the creator can't or won't do something the brief asks for, ask the brand whether it's a dealbreaker before the creator films; only once the brand agrees does the requirement come out. Never drop or change a requirement silently.
Source: coaching call "August 18th - Brands Q&A (Osama)" (2026-08-18) — "Once they say, no, it's not a deal breaker, then of course, just send it to the guy without the jazz music."

### BRD-DELIV-09 — A window for the brand, a firm date for the creator

Rule (should): Give the brand a posting window ('by the end of August', or the 15th to the 25th) and the creator a firm, earlier deadline. Editing and uploads slip; the gap absorbs it without breaking the promise to the brand.
Source: coaching call "July 15th: Brands Q&A with Himzo" (2026-07-15) — "what I like to do with the brands as well is to not really give them like exact dates, um, if I don't have to, I'll try and give them like timeframes or like a month, like, you know, the month of August"
Source: coaching call "July 15th: Brands Q&A with Himzo" (2026-07-15) — "like now I'm locking in the deal, I'll tell them like, look, we can get it, you know, by end of August, and then with the influencer, I'd give them like a deadline of like the 20th of August"

### BRD-DELIV-10 — Relay the creator's words in professional terms

Rule (should): Pass on what the creator needs or objects to in neutral, professional words. Never forward their complaints or harsh remarks about the brand as they wrote them, and never change what they mean.
Source: coaching call "May 06: Student Success Breakdown - Abu Numaan" (2026-05-06) — "don't be like a bank guy that just takes exactly what the influencer says, writes in his words and just says it to the brand. Don't do that. Use your brain."
