# Influencers — After the campaign

### INF-AFTER-01 — Collect the campaign analytics

Rule (must): After the content goes live, ask the creator for the numbers — engagement, impressions and reach, views, click-throughs — as screenshots or a sheet that is easy to share.
Source: 3.5. Results and reviews — "Reach out to the influencer and request data on the campaign. This includes key metrics such as: - Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares) - Impressions and reach - Video views (if applicable) - Click-through rates (if you included links or CTAs in the post) - Make sure to ask for these analytics in a format that is easy to share and interpret (e.g., screenshots, Excel files, etc.)."

### INF-AFTER-02 — 30-day data from the creator, per platform

Rule (must): At 30 days, ask the creator for the non-public numbers: YouTube watch time, average view duration, link CTR; Instagram reach, impressions, accounts engaged, saves, shares; TikTok views, average watch time, reach, likes, comments, shares. Screenshots are fine.
Source: 4.5. Post Campaign Delivery - Coach Michael — "At the 30-day mark, ask the creator for the non-public data. Pull watch time, average view duration, and link CTR on YouTube. Pull reach, impressions, accounts engaged, saves, and shares on Instagram. Pull views, average watch time, reach, likes, comments, and shares on TikTok. Screenshots are fine."
