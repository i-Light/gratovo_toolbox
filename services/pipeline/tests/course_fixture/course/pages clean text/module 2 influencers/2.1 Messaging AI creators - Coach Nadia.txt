Messaging AI creators for the first time

A creator who reviews AI tools gets pitched all week, so your first email has one job: earn a reply.

Open with one detail from their latest upload. The creator should feel that a real person watched the whole video before writing to them.

Keep the first email short, and end it on a single question they can answer in one line.

Never name a sponsor in your opening line. Talk about their channel first and the brand second.
