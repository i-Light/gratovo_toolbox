What it does well

  • Follows the course's structure: one honest point from the video, a clean bridge to the offer, one easy yes-or-no question.
  • Stays honest about brands not yet pitched, and gates every price, term, claim and ethics question to a person.
  • Handles bounces, unsubscribes and out-of-office replies without a Gemini call; after a “no” it schedules a reconnect about 60 working days out instead of chasing.

Where it is weakest

  • Voice. The one graduate win: “sounds like a real person texting a creator instead of a stiff AI summary”.
  • The same phrases in nearly every batch draft: “we're onboarding creators”, “could be a good fit”, “Speaking of…”. The repeated-phrase check needs the unsent batch, which only the app can supply.
  • First emails see a video's title and description, not a moment from it (subtitles decision is yours).

Capacity, roughly

  • About 15–17 two-pass drafts an hour per Gemini account, 25–35 one-pass replies.
  • Around 150–200 drafts a day across both accounts (an estimate from the run logs, not measured at team scale).
  • The mailbox runs out first: the course default is about 50 sends a day per mailbox, 35 of them outreach (HC-02).

Known risks

  • Accounts wear out after about 9 hours of steady use a day; the last run stopped when tabs could not confirm “high” thinking.
  • The add-on sometimes reports “send not accepted” when the send went through (7 times in one run), wasting a generation.
  • No “Gemini is down” state in the app; when it fails, the team writes by hand.
