# Product

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [product.md](product.md) | The product record: users, purpose, constraints and principles. |
| [owner-rules.md](owner-rules.md) | The owner's standing rules and preferences, with reasons. |

Their results are built into the old app and described in `legacy/docs/plans/decisions_build_plan.md`. |

The design itself is the prototype: [design/workbench-prototype/](../../design/workbench-prototype/README.md). Its Plan page (`js/plan.js`) holds the principles, the coverage of every task, the quality-of-life list, the feedback map, the lag causes, the roadmap and the open questions.
