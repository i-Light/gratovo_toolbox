# Influencers — Payment

### INF-PAY-01 — Trigger the creator's payment on go-live day

Rule (must): Start the creator's payment the day the post goes live and track the payout window (about 30 days for flat deals, 35-40 for CPM deals).
Source: 4.4. Campaign Delivery - Coach Michael — "Trigger the creator's payment the same day the post goes live, and track the payout window: 30 days for standard deals, 35 to 40 days for CPM."
Source: lesson video "4.3. Onboarding the Creators - Coach Ammar" @2:35 — "The most common one, a video goes live, you send the invoice from the day of you sending the invoice 30 days payment will be there."

### INF-PAY-02 — Creator terms: Net 30 from go-live, paid once the brand has paid

Rule (should): The creator's contract says Net 30 from go-live even when the brand pays faster, so a slow brand never leaves us owing money we haven't received. Invoice the brand the day the video is live (BRD-PAY-03), and pay the creator as soon as the brand's money is in: paying well before the 30 days is goodwill creators remember. Paying out of pocket before the brand pays is a risk the owner decides on.
Source: coaching call "August 11: Q&A - Michael" (2025-08-11) — "I would invoice the brand as soon as possible, and then you get paid from the brand, and then you are going to send the rate that you agreed with the influencer, you're going to send him that once you get the money"
Source: coaching call "7/1 - Deal Breakdown & Strategy w/Abu Numaan" (2026-01-07) — "even if the brand are paying net 14, make a net 30 with influencer, and pay them as soon as possible, of course, if you need cash flow or something, cool, but usually, the faster you pay influencers, the more happy they are"

### INF-PAY-03 — A creator asks to be paid up front

Rule (should): Explain plainly that payment follows publication, within 30 days, because brands pay after the video is live. If they insist, ask the brand whether it pays part up front (BRD-CONTRACT-03), or offer half once the draft is approved and half when it goes live. Never pay for work that hasn't been delivered by a creator we haven't worked with before.
Source: coaching call "September 8: Q&A -Younes" (2025-09-08) — "The nature of our model, you know, we can't do it. The best we can do is get the payment through within 30 days."
Source: coaching call "September 8: Q&A -Younes" (2025-09-08) — "If he still wants to do it, then you can try it with the brand. Because some brands are okay with actually doing 50% up front."
